PLEASE READ FIRST

this FAQ covers the most common suggestions. please check it before submitting so duplicate requests don't pile up. it also explains where to find features that already exist.

Q: Can you add more songs?
A: More songs increase hosting costs. There is a donation goal that helps cover them, and that's how I decide when new songs can be added. If you want to rank your own songs, there is a custom song ranker you can use: songRanker-local. Drop in any folder of audio and rank it locally. Note: it is very work in progress and very buggy, and there is no global ranking, everything stays in your browser. If you run into any bugs, please report them through this website. Thanks for understanding.
Q: Can you add a joke / meme feature?
A: The site already has a few joke features and I'm keeping the focus on ranking for now to avoid clutter.
Q: Can I hide the accuracy bar?
A: Yes. You can hide it in Settings.
Q: Can you change the ranking algorithm? It takes a long time to reach 100%.
A: You don't need to reach 100% to get a good result. The ranker uses an ELO system, so even a couple hundred comparisons will give you a useful sorted list. It's not a knockout tournament, it's an ongoing comparison system where more votes gradually improve accuracy. You can stop whenever you are happy with the result.

Note: the top of your list (roughly the top 10) tends to stabilize much faster than the full list. You may have an accurate top 10 within a few hundred votes even if the bar shows 20%, since the site prioritizes songs near your top. The remaining comparisons mainly refine lower positions.
Q: Can I rank only boss themes, area themes, or a specific subset of songs?
A: Yes. In Deltarune mode, use "Mix Chapters..." in the filter dropdown to combine specific chapters. In Combined mode, use "Mix Franchises..." to choose which games to include and "Mix DR Chapters..." for Deltarune chapters. For fully custom sets (like only boss themes), click the "+ Custom List" button next to the filter to create a custom playlist where you can pick individual songs.
Q: Can I combine and rank Undertale and Deltarune chapters together?
A: Yes, but Combined mode is hidden at first. Click the "VS" between the two songs three times quickly to unlock the "Combined" tab at the top. Then use "Mix Franchises..." in the filter to choose which games to include (Deltarune, Undertale, UT Yellow, TS!Underswap) and "Mix DR Chapters..." to pick specific Deltarune chapters. You can also create a custom playlist with "+ Custom List" for any exact combination across games.

Please note: Combined mode is very buggy and very unfinished, so use it at your own risk.
Q: Does this feature already exist?
A: Please check Settings and the filter dropdown first. Many requested features are already available there.
Q: Why is a lyrical version of a song playing?
A: That's the Felfeb collaboration. There is about a 10% chance of playing a fanmade lyrical version instead of the original. You can turn this off in Settings. The lyrical versions were made by Felfeb (with permission) and he is not in the server.
Q: Can you add a skip button?
A: The skip button is already there, between the two choice buttons in the center of the arena.
